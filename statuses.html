Trip statuses

The status is the column a trip sits in. It tells you where each client is, and from Booked onward it drives which workflows apply and when the trip moves on its own.

StatusWorkflowNotes
Discovery
Trip InquiriesWebsite form submitted; discovery call booked and held; recap sent.—Moved by hand to Planning when the planning fee and forms are received.
Proposal
PlanningFee paid and forms in. Proposal built in Tern within 3–5 business days.—No workflow yet.
Proposal is ReadyProposal sent. Follow-up, revisions and approval happen here.—In Tern but not in your process notes. Confirm you move trips here when the proposal goes out.
Booking
BookedDeposit placed. Moved by hand from Planning (or Proposal is Ready).All Trips | Booked
Payment Reminders with status updates		Both workflows are set to apply on this status. Trip tasks start here.
Booked (Paid in Full)Final payment placed. Moved by hand from Booked.—The payments workflow, already on the trip, keeps running.
Pre-trip & travel
Trip Prep (30 days)Final 30 days: confirmations, documents, Bon Voyage.Payment RemindersSet automatically 30 days before the trip starts. The automation is named “Trip Status = Prep to Travel”; rename it to match.
TravelingOn the trip.Payment RemindersSet automatically on the trip start date.
TraveledHome again. Welcome Home + survey.Payment RemindersSet automatically on the trip end date.
Closed
CancelledTrip did not go ahead.—Set by hand. Remove the payments workflow so reminders stop.
ArchivedTrip complete.Payment RemindersSet automatically 7 days after the trip ends. Google Review (30 days) and the future-trip follow-up are scheduled after this; see To Review.
Moves that happen on their own

Once the payments workflow is on a trip, Tern moves it to Trip Prep (30 days) 30 days out, Traveling on the start date, Traveled on the end date and Archived 7 days later. The moves before that (Trip Inquiries → Planning → Proposal is Ready → Booked → Booked (Paid in Full)) are made by hand, and Cancelled is set by hand.
